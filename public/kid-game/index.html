Kid Games

Little games that make maths feel like play.

Seven browser games for young learners, from making ten to times tables. Pick one, tap play, and learn by playing.

  • Free to play
  • No sign-up, no ads
  • Runs in the browser

  • Ages 4 to 6

    Make Ten Adventure 凑十大冒险

    Fill the ten-frames to solve sums like 8 + 3, with an owl guide who speaks English and Chinese.

    • Make ten
    • Adding past ten
    • English and 中文
    Tap or click Play

  • Ages 4 to 6

    Ninja Math Dojo

    Throw ninja stars at the lantern with the right answer. Every question is read aloud, and five right answers earn the next karate belt.

    • Counting
    • Adding
    • Taking away
    Tap or click Play

  • Ages 4 to 7

    Make Ten & Friends

    Three quick games in one: find the number that makes ten, count ladybug doubles, then bridge through ten.

    • Number bonds
    • Doubles
    • Bridge to ten
    Tap or click Play

  • Ages 4 to 7

    Make-Ten Ninja

    A little platformer: run, jump and grab the star that finishes the sum. Includes a Make-Twenty Dinosaur level for older players.

    • Make ten
    • Make twenty
    Keys or on-screen buttons Play

  • Ages 5 to 8

    Ninja Math Worlds

    Four games in one: take-away in a 3D dojo, stomp the right answer, count burger boxes for times tables, and drop dino eggs to add.

    • Taking away
    • Adding
    • Times tables
    Tap or click Play

  • Ages 5 and up

    Do Not Touch the Color

    Listen for the call, “Touch green!” or “Do not touch green!”, and run your ninja around the playground to get it right.

    • Listening
    • Colors
    • Quick thinking
    Needs a keyboard Play

  • Ages 8 and up

    Times Strike

    A foam-dart academy for times tables: read the sum on the keypad and tag the training dummy wearing the answer. No blood, no real guns.

    • Times tables 2 to 12
    • Skip counting
    Keyboard and mouse, or touch Play
