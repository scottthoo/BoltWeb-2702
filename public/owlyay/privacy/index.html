Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how BOLT INTERACTIVE PLT (LLP0019699-LGN) (“we”, “us”, or “Bolt Interactive”), a limited liability partnership registered in Malaysia, handles information in connection with Owlyay (Android, application identifier com.boltstudio.owlyay; and iPhone and iPad).

1. What Owlyay is

Owlyay is a parental tool. A parent or guardian installs and sets it up on their child’s phone or tablet. After a viewing time the parent chooses, in a video app the parent selects (such as YouTube), the child sees a short maths question and answers it to keep watching. Owlyay never modifies, embeds, reads or controls YouTube or any other app; it only decides whether a selected app may stay in front.

2. Short version

Everything Owlyay uses stays on the device. We do not collect, receive, sell or share any personal information about you or your child. There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics.

3. Information stored on the device

To do its job, Owlyay keeps the following on the device only. It is never uploaded to us or to any third party by the app:

  • the parent’s settings: viewing time, pause time after a wrong answer, maths level, which apps are watched;
  • the names (package names) of the watched apps and how long they have been in the foreground;
  • a one-way hash of the parent PIN (the PIN itself is not stored);
  • an event log of what Owlyay did (for example, when a learning break started and whether the child answered). It contains watched-app names, times and Owlyay’s own status messages; it does not contain screen contents, what the child watched, names, or contact details.

On Android this data is excluded from cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. Deleting the app, or clearing its data, removes it.

4. No network, accounts, ads or analytics

Android: the app does not request the Internet permission and contains no networking, advertising, analytics or crash-reporting code, so it cannot send information off the device on its own.

iPhone and iPad: the app and its Screen Time extensions contain no networking, advertising, analytics or crash-reporting code. Screen Time and Family Controls data is handled by Apple’s Screen Time framework on the device; apps you choose are represented to Owlyay by Apple as opaque tokens, and Owlyay cannot see your child’s apps, browsing or Screen Time data beyond what the framework enforces for the rules you set. That information stays on the device.

Google Play, the Apple App Store and TestFlight may provide us with aggregated installation or crash statistics under their own terms and privacy policies. These do not identify you or your child to us.

5. Sharing the event log (optional)

The Parent screen has a “Send event log” action. It only works when a parent taps it: Owlyay prepares the log file and opens the system share sheet, and the parent chooses where it goes (for example, an email to us when we are helping with a problem). Nothing is sent unless the parent does this. Anything you share is used only to investigate the issue you reported and is deleted when no longer needed.

6. Permissions and why Owlyay asks for them

On Android, a parent grants these in Settings:

  • Usage access: to see which app is in the foreground and for how long. Owlyay cannot see inside apps.
  • Display over other apps: to bring the maths question to the front during a learning break.
  • Notifications, a foreground service and start at boot: to show that Owlyay is counting viewing time, to remind the child of a break, and to keep working after the device restarts.

On iPhone and iPad, the parent grants Screen Time access (Family Controls) so Owlyay can cover the apps the parent picked after the viewing time. These permissions are used only for the feature described, and the parent can withdraw them at any time in the device’s Settings.

7. Children’s privacy

Owlyay is set up and managed by a parent or guardian, and the child-facing screens are limited to a short maths question, the answer, and a result. We do not collect personal information from children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone through the app. Because no data is sent to us, there is nothing for us to share, sell, or use for advertising, and no child profile is built. This is consistent with COPPA (United States), GDPR Article 8 and the UK Age Appropriate Design Code, and Malaysia’s Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (as amended). If you believe a child has sent us personal information, for example by sharing an event log, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Data retention

We do not hold your data. Data on the device stays there until the parent resets the app, clears its data, or uninstalls it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to or restrict processing. Because we do not hold personal data about you or your child, these are satisfied by deleting the app or its data on the device. If you have shared an event log with us and want it deleted, or have any question, email us at boltstudiogames@gmail.com.
