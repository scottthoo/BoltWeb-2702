Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how BOLT INTERACTIVE PLT (LLP0019699-LGN) (“we”, “us”, or “Bolt Interactive”), a limited liability partnership registered in Malaysia, handles information in connection with the mobile game Sling Bowl (iOS and Android; application identifier com.boltstudio.slingbowl).

1. Information we collect

Sling Bowl has no accounts or sign-in. We, the developer, do not collect personal data from you when you play the game.

Your progress and settings are stored locally on your device. That information stays on the device and is not uploaded to our servers.

2. Advertising

The game uses Google AdMob to show interstitial ads (between stages) and rewarded ads (for example, an optional extra ball). Google AdMob may collect device identifiers such as IDFA on iOS and AAID on Android, IP address, coarse location, and ad interaction and diagnostic data. Google uses this information for advertising, measurement, and fraud prevention.

You can read Google’s privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy and how Google uses data from partner apps at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

3. Consent and advertising choices

Where required, we present consent choices through Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP), including for users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and other regions where this is required. On iOS, the app may also show Apple’s App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt.

You can opt out of personalized ads through your device settings. On iOS, this includes Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, and Apple’s advertising settings. On Android, this includes your Google Ads or privacy settings. Choosing not to see personalized ads, or declining tracking, does not necessarily remove all ads.

4. In-app purchases

Sling Bowl offers an optional in-app purchase called “Remove Ads”. Purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Play Store). We do not receive or store your payment card details.

Buying Remove Ads turns off interstitial ads. Rewarded ads may remain available as an optional feature you can choose to use.

5. Children’s privacy

Sling Bowl is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the game or our advertising partners, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it where it is within our control.

6. Data retention

Because we do not collect personal data ourselves, we do not retain personal data on our servers. Progress and settings remain on your device until you delete the app or clear the app’s data. Advertising and payment partners may retain information according to their own policies.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data, or to object to or restrict certain processing. Because we do not hold personal data about you, most requests relating to gameplay can be fulfilled by deleting the app or clearing its local data on your device.

To make an access or deletion request, or to ask a question about this policy, email us at boltstudiogames@gmail.com. For information held by Google in connection with ads, you may also use the controls and rights described in Google’s privacy policy.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date at the top of this page and post the revised policy at this URL: https://bolt-interactive.com/sling-bowl/privacy.
